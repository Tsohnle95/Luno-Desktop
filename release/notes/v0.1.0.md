### What’s included

The first public preview of the React, Tauri, and Rust desktop app.

- Browse your local library by playlist or album, search songs, and save favorites.
- Play music with a queue, shuffle, repeat, and macOS media keys.
- Discover recommendations, preview songs, and save them to your library.
- Download songs and sync YouTube playlists, with sequential downloads and review for unavailable songs.
- Edit song metadata and artwork, and watch available music videos.
- Select multiple songs with a long press and review duplicate copies before deleting files.
- Transfer your library with the shared Luno Desktop and Luno Mobile export format.
- Use transient overlay scrollbars that stay hidden when idle and leave more room for your music.

### Install or update

1. Download the DMG on an Apple Silicon Mac.
2. Open it and drag **Luno Desktop** into **Applications**.
3. Open **Luno Desktop** from Applications.
4. If macOS blocks the unsigned app, review [Apple’s instructions for opening an unidentified developer’s app](https://support.apple.com/102445). If you choose to proceed, use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** after attempting to open it.

For an existing Tauri installation, quit Luno before replacing the app in Applications. Keep your music folders and application data; replacing the app does not require deleting them. Back up your library before trying a preview release.

The app includes its reviewed FFmpeg, ffprobe, and yt-dlp helpers; no separate installation of those tools is required. Online downloads and discovery need an internet connection. Recommendation discovery requires a Last.fm API key configured in Luno’s settings.
